CREATE VIEW rider_readable AS
SELECT
  ride_id,
  bike_type,

  datetime(end_time, 'unixepoch') AS end_time,

    WHEN 1 THEN 'customer'

  END AS user_type,
  CASE gender

    WHEN 1 THEN 'female'
    ELSE 'unknown'
  END AS gender,
  birth_year
FROM rides

The data from Midway contains no entries for snow, peak wind gust or total sunsine (minutes). So we can drop those columns. Also most people do not consciously notice changes in air pressure, unless it's extreme. Factors like precipitation, temperature and wind speed, have a far stronger and more direct influence on bike usage. While air pressure might correlate with some weather conditions (e.g., low pressure with storms), it's not the pressure itself that influences behavior—it's the weather phenomena that accompany it. So we’ll be dropping air pressure as well.
